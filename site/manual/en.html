MANUAL / @VERSION@

Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 for dark industrial and EBM: four tracks, a sequencer with locks, drums, distortion and live effects.

KRIECHSTROM is built on Felucca 1.8.2 by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), GPL-3.0. The GLCH engine, the DESTROY kit, the MUFF / BASS / SMASH pedals and the ROLL and GATE effects come from Glitchbox by erikdevos. Glitchbox itself runs only on the older FM-1 flash chip; KRIECHSTROM brings these parts to the newer units. Everything Felucca does still works: this manual covers what you need to play, and what KRIECHSTROM added.

Install

  1. Connect the FM-1 by USB. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (the installer needs Web MIDI).
  2. Open the installer, press Connect, then Install. Do not unplug until it says done.
  3. The FM-1 restarts with the KRIECHSTROM splash. On the computer it shows up as KRIECHSTROM (MIDI and audio).

Tested on the FM-1 with the new flash chip. Your Felucca projects load as they are. To go back to the official firmware, the installer has a Restore option.

First five minutes

  1. Press PLAY. A beat and a bass start.
  2. Turn ALGORITHM to pick a track. The number in the top left corner is the track.
  3. Turn PRESETS: the sound of that track changes while it plays. The pattern stays.
  4. Play the keys: they play the selected track.
  5. Hold SEQ and press F5 (DICE): the track gets a new pattern in the current mood. Press again for another.
  6. Hold FX and tap a white key: repeat, filter, tape stop and more go on. Tap it again: off. OCT− with FX held: all off.
  7. Didn't like it? Hold SAVE: undo.

Changing sounds

PRESETS

Turn PRESETS anywhere. Tap SAVE twice for the PRESETS list: K2 picks the engine, K3 marks a favourite, K4 shows one category only (BASS, LEAD, PAD, PLUCK, KEYS, DRUM, FX).

Engines

Hold EDIT: the white keys are the engines. Press one and the track switches to it with its first sound.

Editing

Tap EDIT: EDIT 1 and EDIT 2 are the engine's eight parameters on K1-K4. Tap again for VOICE (poly / mono, glide), and the engine's own pages. ENV is the envelope, LFO the LFOs and the mod matrix.

Hold LFO and turn a knob for a MOMENTARY move: let go of LFO and the value jumps back.

The GLCH engine

GLCH is an oscillator (15 waves: SAW, SQR, DUAL, RING, NOIS, FAT, FM, FMX, FUZZ, REES, 808, GRIT, GROW, SYNC, SUPR) into a resonant filter, a clipper and folder, a bit crusher and a micro-stutter that chops the note into pieces. Most of its 48 sounds come with a PEDAL already set (MUFF, BASS, SMASH, FUZZ, FOLD, CRUSH).

KnobEDIT 1EDIT 2
K1WAVECRSH: bit crush
K2CUT: filter cutoffGLCH: how often the stutter cuts in
K3RES: resonance, up to self-oscillationSIZE: the stutter's slice length
K4DRV: drive into the clipperPJMP: pitch jumps between slices

HAPPY ACCIDENT (OOPS)

Hold EDIT and press G5. Two or three of the sound's parameters jump somewhere else, sometimes an effect send too. The screen says what moved, for example OOPS: CUT RES DLY. Press again for more. Kept something good? Save it (SAVE > USER). Went too far? Hold SAVE: the sound comes back as it was before the first OOPS.

It never touches the level, the pattern, the engine or the kit, so the track keeps playing as part of the song.

The sequencer

Each track has its own pattern, up to 64 steps. Tap SEQ for STEP. On a synth track STEP is a piano roll.

Writing notes: step by step

  1. Stop. Go to SEQ > STEP. Press REC to arm the track.
  2. Play a key (or a chord: hold the keys together). The note goes to the step under the cursor and the cursor moves on.
  3. K1 moves the cursor (STEP), K2 changes the note, K3 the length (hold over several steps = a tie), K4 the flags: ACCENT and SLIDE.
  4. Press REC again to disarm.

Writing notes: live

Press PLAY, then REC, and play. The loop records what you play, overdubbing every pass. Your timing is kept as a NUDGE; QUANTIZE on the SCL page decides if it is played as you played it or on the grid. Hold REC for the click and a count-in.

Locks: one step, its own value

On STEP hold a step (its key) and turn any knob: that value is set for that step only. A dot marks steps with locks. Hold the step and press EDIT to clear its locks.

DETAIL and AUTOMATION

Tap SEQ again for DETAIL: the cursor step's CHANCE (also conditions like 1:2, 3:4 and FILL), RATCHET (2-4 hits in a step), NUDGE and VELOCITY. PRESETS moves the cursor here. Once more for AUTOMATION: a list of everything that happens on the steps, locks and recorded knob moves, which you can edit or delete one by one.

Length, speed, swing

Hold SEQ: K1 LEN (1-64 steps), K2 DIV (4 bars to 1/32), K3 SWING, K4 GATE. Odd lengths on different tracks drift against each other: 16 on the drums, 14 on the bass, 7 on a line.

Drums

On a DRUM track STEP is a grid: white keys are steps 1-16, black keys pick the lane (kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, tom, rim, bell). Pick a lane with a black key, then press white keys to set or clear its hits. K1 moves along the steps (pages of 16), K2 picks the lane, K3 the hit, K4 the accent.

Locks work here too: hold a step key and turn a knob. Hold EDIT on a drum track: the black keys mute lanes. The kits: DRUM KIT, DEEP KIT, TIGHT KIT, PERC KIT, DESTROY (from Glitchbox, with the SMASH pedal), and the KRIECHSTROM kits FACTORY, STOMP, GATED, BUNKER, TOMB, RIOT, METAL SHOP, DRY BONES. Change the kit with PRESETS like any sound.

DICE and moods

DICE writes a part that fits the track: on a drum track a beat, on track 1 a bass line, on track 2 stabs and chords, on tracks 3 and 4 a high line, riser or drone. Notes are in the track's scale and root (hold SCL and press a key to change the root). Every press is different. MOOD picks what kind of part:

MoodFeel
MACHINEstraight sixteenths on the root, kick on every beat, hats on the offbeat
GRINDslower: the bass slides to the flat second, low held chords
MARCH 7/814 steps, accented notes on every other step
GLITCHrandom hits and notes, gaps in the beat
SPIRALdescending lines, tom rolls
GHOSTsparse: a held root, an arpeggio, a single bell
SURGEdense: offbeat bass, open hats, claps, a riser
HUSHquiet: eighth notes in the bass, a held chord, a drone

The mood is not saved: the FM-1 starts with MACHINE.

Live: the FX layer

Hold FX and tap a white key: its effect goes on for the whole mix and its cell lights. Tap it again: off. OCT− with FX held turns every effect off. Black keys mute tracks. K1-K4 are macros: FILTER, CRUSH, THROW, DEPTH. Turn SELECT with FX held for WALL (see DAMAGE). If you would rather have an effect only while a key is held, set MENU > CONTROL > FX LATCH to OFF.

Mutes, solos, fills, songs

Hold GLO: black keys F#3-C#4 mute tracks 1-4, white keys F3-B3 solo while held, C4 unmutes all, D4 MIDI LEARN, E4 FILL while held (steps with the FILL condition play), F4 TAP tempo. K1-K4 are the track levels.

Tap GLO for SONG: up to 16 sections, each plays the project slots A-D on every track, with repeats.

Effects: what is what

Tap FX again and again: the pages go round. The big line under the knobs says what the page does. Every value that turns an effect off reads OFF at zero.

PageWhat it isOff
SENDShow much of this track goes into distortion, chorus, delay and reverb (K1-K4)a knob at OFF
SLICERchops this track in time: GATE or STUTTER, in time with the tempoK1 OFF

PEDAL MIXhow much of the pedal you hear, dry to wetOFF
DELAYone delay shared by all tracks: TIME, FEEDBACK, COLOUR, MIXMIX OFF (all tracks), or a track's DLY send

CHORUSone chorus for all tracksa track's CHO send

Delay, reverb and chorus are shared: a track gets them through its SENDS. That is why the delay "does not turn off" when you change the sound: the preset's sound has its own DLY send. Set DELAY > MIX to OFF to silence the delay for everyone, or SENDS > DLY to OFF for one track.

DAMAGE and WALL

The last FX page, four separate things:

  • DMG (K1) degrades the whole mix: drive, then, as CHAR (K2) goes from tape to digital, a darker tone, the pitch wobbling and dips like a stretched tape, or lost bits, a dropped sample rate and hard cuts.
  • WALL (K3) is one knob from quiet to wall, with no wear in it: it pushes every track's distortion and effect sends up, adds a heavily compressed copy of the mix under it and widens the stereo, and from 80 % on it plays the FILL steps. Turn it while playing: hold FX and turn SELECT.
  • HISS (K4) is a tape's noise floor, on its own: it hisses while the tape runs, that is while PLAY plays, and when stopped while you play and for a second after. Stopped and silent: none. OFF: never.

From a controller: CC85 is WALL, CC86 DMG. The pedal DAMG does the same wear on one track (AMT, CHAR, LEVEL).

Saving and undo

  • Undo: hold SAVE. Works for sound loads, tools, DICE, OOPS, clearing.
  • A sound: stop, tap SAVE (USER), pick a slot with K1, OCT+, name it with the keys, OCT+. 32 slots.
  • The music: SAVE > PROJECT: four projects, K4 saves, K3 loads, K2 erases.
  • Patterns: SAVE > PHRASES loads ready-made patterns into the track.
  • Autosave: turn the FM-1 off and on and the music is as you left it (MENU > RESTORE LAST).

MIDI and USB audio

  • USB and TRS MIDI in: channels 1-4 play tracks 1-4 (MENU > MIDI > MIDI IN for other channels).
  • The keys send MIDI out on the track's channel. Clock from internal, USB or TRS.
  • CCs: 5 glide, 7 level, 10 pan, 71 resonance, 74 brightness, 72/73/75 release/attack/decay, 91/93/94 reverb, chorus, delay. GLCH: 74 cutoff, 71 resonance.
  • MIDI LEARN: hold GLO, press D4, move a knob and a CC on your controller.
  • USB audio: the FM-1 is a stereo audio input called KRIECHSTROM. Record it in any DAW, no driver. KRIECHSTROM ships with the USB serial console off, so macOS 13-15 sees the audio input right away.

Menu and screen

Hold HOME for the menu. ALGORITHM moves between the tabs, PRESETS through the rows, any knob changes the value, HOME closes it. KRIECHSTROM starts with the STROM palette (black and red), LARGE on (big values) and HELP on. For other colours: DISPLAY > COLOR.
