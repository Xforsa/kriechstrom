KRIECHSTROM

Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 for dark industrial and EBM: four tracks, a step sequencer, distorted synths, drums and live effects.

Install on the FM-1 Read the manual Cheat sheet Download kriechstrom-0.3.1.fwsc

What is in it

  • GLCH engine from Glitchbox: clipping, wavefolding, a resonant filter, bit crushing and micro-stutter; 56 sounds, most with a distortion pedal set.
  • 179 sounds in 13 engines, ready to play: distorted basses, cold leads, detuned keys, pads, noise and textures, and 13 drum kits. The list can be filtered by category.
  • DICE: hold SEQ, press F5 and the track gets a new part in one of eight moods, from MACHINE to HUSH.
  • HAPPY ACCIDENT: hold EDIT, press G5 and two or three parameters of the sound change at random. Hold SAVE to undo.
  • DAMAGE, WALL and HISS on the whole mix (FX > DAMAGE): tape or digital wear with dropouts; a wall of sound (everything double-tracked, an octave added under the low end, squeezed hard); tape hiss while the tape runs. The level stays where it was. Two bars dry, then DAMAGE on tape, then digital. Two bars dry, then WALL.
  • INFECT, a sidechain pedal (PEDAL > INFCT): choose another track as the source (SRC) and this track reacts to it. MODE:
    • GATE: the track plays only while the source hits, a choppy gate from your kick;
    • DUCK: it dips on every hit and swells back, the pumping pad;
    • CRUSH and FOLD: every hit distorts it;
    • RING: the two tracks multiplied, a metallic ring;
    • VOX: a vocoder, the drums (or any track) talk through the pad. The same sidechain, in 8 frequency bands.
    The source can sit at LEVEL 0: you hear only what it does to the other track, like a hidden kick for sidechain. Two bars dry, then a silent kick pumps the pad (DUCK), then the drums crush the bass. Two bars dry, then the drums speak through the pad (VOX).
  • HOWL and MIC in GLCH: no oscillator, a loop that feeds itself. HOWL is a string in overdrive that sustains and screams in tune; MIC is a microphone in front of a speaker that howls at the note. Two bars dry, then the melody turns into FEEDBACK, later SCREAMER.
  • RESONATE on the whole mix: four strings tuned to a chord (KEY, CHORD), rung by everything that plays. Two bars dry, then RESONATE in A minor: listen to the silences.
  • SPLICE on the whole mix: the tape cut and glued back wrong. On the sixteenths a piece is glued in instead of what plays: the last sixteenth again or backwards, a piece from a beat ago, the tape dragged an octave down, or nothing at all. One knob for how often and how hard. Two bars dry, then SPLICE, harder from bar 6.
  • A 64-step sequencer per track with locks, conditions, ratchets, automation and songs.
  • Drums with 13 kits, DESTROY included, eight lanes, a grid on the keys.
  • Live effects on the keys: repeats, tape stop, freeze, filter sweeps, ROLL, GATE, reverb and delay throws.
  • MIDI in over USB and TRS, MIDI learn, USB audio. Shows up on the computer as KRIECHSTROM.
  • Black and red, big readable values, hints on screen.

Kriechstrom is German for creepage current: the leakage across worn insulation that leaves a branching mark like this one, a Lichtenberg figure.

Why

Glitchbox is built on Felucca 1.5.1 for the older FM-1 flash chip and does not run on the newer units. KRIECHSTROM is built on the current Felucca, which supports both, and brings over the GLCH engine and the effects from Glitchbox.

Built on

KRIECHSTROM is built on Felucca 1.8.2 by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments, with parts of Glitchbox by erikdevos and ideas from DIRGE by sinscum. Thanks to all three for the inspiration. It is free software under the GNU GPL 3.0: the source of this build (6.9 MB).
